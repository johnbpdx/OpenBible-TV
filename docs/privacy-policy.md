# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 12, 2026  
**App:** OpenBible TV (Roku Channel)  
**Developer:** John Burton

---

## Overview

OpenBible TV is designed with your privacy in mind. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

OpenBible TV does **not** collect:

- Names, email addresses, or account information
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Crash reports or telemetry
- Any other personally identifiable information (PII)

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

---

## Third-Party Services

OpenBible TV does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.  
The app does not make network requests and does not transmit data to any external server.

---

## Bible Content

The Bible text displayed in OpenBible TV is sourced from the public domain (King James Version or World English Bible). No personal data is involved in accessing this content.

---

## Children's Privacy

OpenBible TV does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no data from any user, it is safe for all ages.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
