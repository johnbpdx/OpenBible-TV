# OpenBible TV

**A free, open-source Bible study app for Roku streaming devices.**

Bring Scripture to your living room. OpenBible TV lets you browse all 66 books of the Bible, read chapter by chapter, and grow in your faith — right on your TV.

---

## Features

- **Browse all 66 books** of the Old and New Testaments
- **Color-coded by testament** — Old Testament and New Testament at a glance
- **Scrollable chapter & verse navigation** with your Roku remote
- **Public-domain Bible text** — no subscriptions, no accounts, no ads
- **Free and open-source** — always

---

## How to Install (Beta)

OpenBible TV is currently available as a beta channel. To install:

1. Visit [my.roku.com](https://my.roku.com) and sign in
2. Go to **My Account → Add channel with a code**
3. Enter the channel access code *(coming soon)*
4. Click **Add channel** and confirm
5. Open the Roku Home screen — OpenBible TV will appear in your channel list
